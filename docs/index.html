Privacy Policy — ARCH

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Overview

ARCH ("we", "our", "us") is a calisthenics training application built for Android devices. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — collected when you create an account via email or Google Sign-In
  • Display name — provided by you during onboarding

Workout Data

  • Workout sessions — exercises completed, sets, reps, duration, and XP earned
  • Fitness profile — fitness goal and level selected during onboarding
  • Streak and progress data — training frequency and progression metrics

Device Information

  • Motion sensor data — accelerometer readings used solely for the auto rep counter feature during active workouts. This data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — we use Sentry (sentry.io) for crash reporting and error monitoring. Sentry may automatically collect diagnostic data including device model, operating system version, and error stack traces to help us identify and fix app issues. This data is not used for any purpose other than improving app stability.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the ARCH training experience
  • To track your workout progress and calculate XP/leveling
  • To provide personalized workout recommendations
  • To enable the auto rep counter feature using motion sensors

Data Storage

  • All workout data is stored locally on your device using secure device storage
  • Account data is managed through Supabase, a third-party authentication and database service
  • We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes

Data Retention

  • Workout data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Account data is retained until you delete your account
  • You can sign out and clear local data at any time from the Profile screen

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data in the app's History and Profile screens
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app removes all local data. To delete your account data, contact us at the email below
  • Portability: Your workout history is visible and can be manually exported

Children's Privacy

ARCH is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
